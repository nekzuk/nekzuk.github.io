01 Features

Fix, finish, and process in batches.

Tools designed primarily for editing AI-generated images, in one app.

AI-assisted cleanup

Fix problem areas right where you see them.

  • AI inpaintingTwo kinds: lightweight inpainting that fills from the surroundings, and generative inpainting that redraws from a prompt (see below)
  • Diff replacementFor fixing fine details
  • Mosaic / blurAdjust or add mosaics after AutoMosaic
  • Editing toolsSAM2 auto masks, pen, eraser, fill, text / speech bubbles, asset insertion

Correct and convert

Improve color and quality, then export as needed.

  • AI correction & color adjustmentAutomatic AI correction and manual color adjustment
  • Image quality fixesDenoise, deblur, and fix artifacts and color fringing
  • AI upscaling & resizing
  • Background removal
  • Format conversion & PDF export

Sort, organize, and batch

Handle large numbers of images at once.

  • Browse, sort & bookmarkReview images in a folder one by one and sort them
  • Tags & captionsGenerate tags and natural-language captions, and bulk-edit tags
  • View / remove metadata
  • Batch processing & CLIProcess a whole folder at once. Background removal, AI correction, conversion, and tags / captions also run from the command line
